HOW IT WORKS

A grant-development system, not a blank chat box.

Each module has a specific job. The output of one module becomes intelligence for the next.

01

Understand the organization

Build a structured profile from uploaded documents, goals, readiness information, and project details.

02

Find and compare opportunities

Surface candidate funders, award information, verification needs, and compatibility indicators.

03

Develop the proposal

Translate strategy into planning drafts, evidence maps, quality checks, and downloadable deliverables.
