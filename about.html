The idea behind Grant Intelligence™

Funding should not begin with paperwork.

Grant Intelligence™ begins with a simpler question: How do you want to help?

Someone may arrive with a nonprofit, a business, a résumé, years of experience—or nothing more than an idea. The platform is designed to identify what already exists, what needs to be developed, and what steps can move an idea closer to a fundable project.

The free assessment is intentionally lightweight. The paid workflow is designed to become progressively more structured, eventually connecting organization intelligence, readiness, funding opportunities, funder alignment, proposal development, and proposal writing.
